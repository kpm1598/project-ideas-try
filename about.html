Meet the Developer

Hi, I'm Kasim Patel

B.Tech CSE (AI & ML) student at Dayananda Sagar University, Bengaluru.

I turn student ideas into working software. Project Ideas is my platform for collecting ideas from fellow students, picking the ones with real potential, and building them — with full credit to the person who dreamed them up.

Submit Your Idea → My GitHub

Why I started Project Ideas

I'm a student who loves building things — apps, websites, AI tools, whatever solves a real problem. Between classes and hackathons (I built LabhSetu with my team, Tech Vedas, at Aarambh), I kept hearing the same thing from other students: "I have this idea for an app…" — and then nothing. The idea stayed in a notebook because they didn't know how to build it.

So I built this platform. Submit your idea, and if it has real potential, I'll take it up — design it, build it, and put it on the Ideas Wall with your name on it, from the day it's submitted to the day it's a working project you can open and use.

I'm one student building in my spare time, so I can't take up every idea — but I read every single submission, and the selected ones get built properly.
